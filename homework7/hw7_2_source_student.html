Platform: Hokie AI · Submission: Thread exported as a .zip and uploaded to Canvas
Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, students will be able to:

  1. Write a GOVERNANCE section with Hokie AI for an agent with three tools, under Authority, Approval, Accountability and Audit, starting from the date the agent cannot know by itself.
  2. Install the section with a tool that sends a request to a manager, and probe the agent under each principle and on a tool of your own design.
  3. Read from Execution Details which rules held, which slipped and which went too far.
  4. Move a rule from the instructions into a tool's schema, and test what the agent does with a limit it can see.
  5. Decide where a rule should live: in the instructions, in the schema, with a manager, or in the business's own system.

CASE TWO

Case two is worth 50 points, 25 for the exchange in the M block and 25 for the reflection in the R block. Stay in the same regular thread.

Every rule you wrote so far lives in the instructions, where the agent reads it and can still slip. Thursday's places card named a second place, the tool's schema. A schema can carry a limit on a value, such as a "maximum" for a number, a "minimum", or an "enum", a list of the only values allowed. In this case you move one rule into a schema and find out what the agent does with it.

Choose a rule from your section that limits a value one of your tools sends: the most rooms or nights for one reservation, the room types, the check-out times your own tool allows, or a limit of your own. The rule you trust least is the best choice when it is a limit of this kind. Then do four things.

First, in the regular thread, paste the opening marker by itself. Copy the schema request below, write your rule after "The rule:" in your own words, copy the tool's current Parameters Schema from its field on your agent's edit page, paste it with the tool's name after the last line, and send it. Hokie AI returns the new schema.

Second, in your next message, say in your own words what changed. Name the property, the keyword Hokie AI added and the value it allows, and whether that matches your rule. Ask for a fix if it does not.

Third, open your agent's edit page. Replace that tool's Parameters Schema with the new one. Then delete the rule's line from the GOVERNANCE section in the Specific tab, so the schema is the only place the rule lives, and save. If the editor refuses the schema, write down its message, because that is a finding.

Fourth, open a new chat with your agent and send two requests of your own that break the limit, one by a little and one by a lot, with the full date and year.
