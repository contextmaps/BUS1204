Week 6 · Lecture 2
Strengthening an Agent's Instructions
Duration: 75 minutes · Platform: Hokie AI · Submission: Strengthening thread exported as a .zip and uploaded to Canvas before leaving class · Lecture Mode: consolidating
Learning Objectives

By the end of this lecture, students will be able to:

  1. Configure an order assistant for a business of their own from a SCORE template and share it with a partner.
  2. Shop a classmate's agent and record the exact message and reply where it gave away a deal or private business information.
  3. Strengthen a weak instruction with Hokie AI as a collaborator by quoting the failure, naming the weak line and stating the business decision the line should carry.
  4. Judge the suggested wording, keep or adjust it, and retest the agent with the message that broke it.
Section 1 of 5
INTRODUCTION
📋 Note for Students

On Tuesday a classmate played your agent and found what your rules left open. Today you learn to close it. You build an agent for a business, your partner shops it, and you strengthen the line that let something slip, with Hokie AI writing the wording to your decision.

The last move is a skill you can use on any agent, any set of instructions and any policy document you will ever write.

Sit with Tuesday's partner. You build an agent for a business, your partner shops it the way a customer would, and you fix what they find.

A shop assistant makes promises in the owner's name. Every rule in its instructions is a business decision, and a vague rule hands that decision to the agent.

Section 4 of 5
DISCUSSION

Hands up if the agent you shopped gave away a deal, then a secret, then both. Then hands for retests that held.

If your retest broke, say what the customer typed the second time. The room will hear what your new line still left open.

Turn to your partner. You shopped their agent and they shopped yours, so each of you saw the other's from the customer's side. Tell your partner what you sent that broke it and what it said back AND what the retest returned with the same message AND one message you would try next against the strengthened version.

Four minutes, both directions.

📋 Note for Students

POTENTIAL USE CASES. Anywhere an agent speaks for someone, a vague rule hands it a decision, and the repair is the same four parts.

A club treasurer's assistant that answers dues questions and never promises a waiver the board has not approved.
A tutoring or lawn care side business whose assistant quotes the posted rate and passes every haggle to you.
A sublease listing assistant that answers questions about the apartment and never shares your rent or your landlord's number.
At an internship, a customer FAQ agent whose line about what to share with clients decides what a client hears.
