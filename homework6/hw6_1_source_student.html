Building and Reviewing an Agent for a Pet Sitting Client
Platform: Hokie AI · Submission: Thread exported as a .zip and uploaded to Canvas
Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, students will be able to:

  1. Interview a new client about a one sentence wish, one element and one question at a time, and ask why when the client states a wish.
  2. Turn an interview transcript into an agent's instructions with Hokie AI, build the agent and test the agent.
  3. Trace an agent's reply on a test to a line in its instructions and to a question asked, or not asked, in the interview.
  4. Take test results back to the client, recommend changes with business reasons, and record what the client approves.
  5. Revise an agent's instructions from the client's approvals and retest the agent with the same tests.
  6. Recognize an approved change that asks an agent to do something the agent cannot do.

CASE TWO

Case two is worth 50 points, 25 for the exchange in the M block and 25 for the reflection in the R block. Stay in the same regular thread.

A consultant does not hand over an agent and leave. Take the test chat back to Maya, show her what the agent did, and recommend changes. Maya reads the results as a business owner. She will agree with some recommendations, push back on others with her reasons, and approve changes one at a time. Her approval decides what goes into the revised instructions.

Open a new chat with Maya, from the same link as the interview. Send the review opening below, then paste the whole test transcript as the next message. Then recommend changes, one per message, each with the test reply that worries you quoted and a reason that matters to Maya's business. When Maya pushes back, ask what she needs and look for a change that keeps the help and removes the risk. End by asking Maya to confirm which changes she approves.

Before you recommend a change, ask yourself whether the agent can actually do what the change asks. An agent can only send words in a chat.
