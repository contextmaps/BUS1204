COMMON QUESTIONS
When should I book for the holidays? Thanksgiving opens for booking on November 1 and winter break on November 15. Holiday weeks fill up fast.
What should I leave out? Food for every day you are away plus two extra days, the leash, litter and bags, and paper towels.
What happens in snow or ice? Visits can move up to two hours earlier or later, and we text you if a visit moves.
Can I tip my sitter? Yes. Every tip goes to the sitter.
Do you sell gift cards? Yes, in any amount. Text us to order one.
