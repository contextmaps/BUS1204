Reviewing a Generated Flyer Before You Send It
Platform: Hokie AI · Submission: Thread exported as a .txt and uploaded to Canvas
Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, students will be able to:

  1. Ask for a promotional flyer for something they invented, supplying almost nothing, and put its words into the thread.
  2. Find a line on the flyer that promises something to a reader and say who has to make good on it.
  3. Set Hokie AI's account of what it invented against the words that are actually on the page.
  4. Decide which lines go out under their own name, and say what a line they kept still needs to be true.
  5. Write a rule before asking for a second version, then find what their own rule failed to cover.

CASE ONE

Case one is worth 50 points, 25 for the exchange in the M block and 25 for the reflection in the R block.

Type one sentence into the box in the block below, then press the copy button and paste the result into your thread. The button wraps your sentence in everything else the block needs, so the only thing you write is the sentence.

Name the business and nothing else. No price, no place, no opening date, no promise about what it does well. If you invented a bakery, a sentence like a bakery I want to open does it. If you invented an app, an app I built for splitting a restaurant bill does it. Twenty-five words is the ceiling and you will not need half of them.

Send it once.

SH2.2.1 Standard Prompt AI: respond
Make a one-page flyer for the following.
Your sentence, 25 words at most.
Make the flyer now from what I have told you, and do not ask me anything first.

Now get the words of that flyer into the thread, because the flyer itself will not travel with your export and the person reading your work cannot see it. Everything you say afterwards depends on those words being on the record.

How you do it depends on what arrived. A flyer that comes back as a page in a panel of its own has a code view you can copy from. A flyer that comes back as an image has to be typed, which is usually under a dozen lines and is worth the two minutes, because a line you type is a line you have read.

Now open the M block below. Everything from here until you close it belongs inside it.

Read the flyer against what you actually know about your business, which is everything, since you invented it. Then three moves, in order.

Find a line on the flyer that promises a reader something. Name the line, say who would have to make good on that promise, and say what happens to a reader who believed it and turned up AND ask Hokie AI what it made up, then set its answer beside the words you put in the thread, tell it what it named that is not on the flyer and what is on the flyer that it did not name, and say whether its answer tells you which of the lines it listed are false AND say which lines you would let go out under your name and which you would cut, and ask whether any line you kept still needs something to be true that you cannot show. All three moves.

The third move is the task. The first two are how you get there.

Eight messages inside the block is the ceiling, and a ceiling is not a target. Chase an answer when the answer is worth chasing.

If the first move stalls, read the flyer one line at a time and stop at every line that a stranger could hold you to. A line that describes your idea is not a promise. A line that tells a reader what they will get is.

CASE TWO

Case two is worth 50 points, 25 for the exchange in the M block and 25 for the reflection in the R block.

Same thread, same invented business, and now you set the terms before you ask for anything. Open the M block below first and send nothing before you have. The order is what is being graded in this case, so your rule has to sit inside the open block and it has to be there before the second flyer is.

Once the block is open, three things, in order.

Write your rule into the thread in your own words, before you ask for anything at all, saying what may appear on something carrying your name, what may not, and what has to be true before a line is allowed on it AND ask for a new flyer built to that rule, then get every line of the second flyer into the thread the same way the cards below show, and go through your rule point by point saying for each point whether it was followed AND find one thing on the new flyer that your rule did not cover and that you would still cut, and ask Hokie AI why your rule did not catch it. All three things.

The third thing is the task. The first two are how you get there.

Eight messages inside the block is the ceiling, and a ceiling is not a target.

Your rule is yours and there is no correct one. A rule nobody could break is not a rule, and a rule that only says be honest settles nothing when a line has to be judged.
