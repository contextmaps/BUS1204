THE RULES YOU NEED
Power rule: the derivative of x^n is n x^(n-1).
Product rule: the derivative of f(x) g(x) is f'(x) g(x) + f(x) g'(x).
Quotient rule: the derivative of f(x) / g(x) is [f'(x) g(x) - f(x) g'(x)] / g(x)^2.
Chain rule: the derivative of f(g(x)) is f'(g(x)) g'(x). Work from the outside in.
Common derivatives: sin x gives cos x, cos x gives -sin x, e^x gives e^x, ln x gives 1/x.

HOW TO START A PROBLEM
1. Ask what the outermost operation is: a product, a quotient, or a function inside a function.
2. Pick the rule for that operation.
3. Write the pieces f and g before you differentiate anything.

PRACTICE PROBLEMS
P1. Find the derivative of x^4 - 3x^2 + 7.
P2. Find the derivative of x^2 e^x.
P3. Find the derivative of (3x + 1)^5.
P4. Find the derivative of sin(x^2).
P5. Find the derivative of (x^2 + 1) / (x - 1).
P6. Find the derivative of ln(4x^3 + x).

ANSWERS
P1. 4x^3 - 6x
P2. 2x e^x + x^2 e^x
P3. 15 (3x + 1)^4
P4. 2x cos(x^2)
P5. [2x (x - 1) - (x^2 + 1)] / (x - 1)^2, which simplifies to (x^2 - 2x - 1) / (x - 1)^2
P6. (12x^2 + 1) / (4x^3 + x)
