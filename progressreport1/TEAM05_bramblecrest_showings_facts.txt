SHOWINGS
Showings run Monday to Saturday, from 9:00 am to the last start time of the day, which is one hour before sunset. Each showing lasts 30 minutes. A buyer books at least 24 hours ahead. A buyer may book up to 4 showings in one day. Every showing is led by a Bramblecrest agent, and buyers enter a home only with the agent.

WHO MAY BOOK
A buyer who is not yet working with a Bramblecrest agent gives a name, phone number and email, and says whether they are preapproved for a mortgage. A buyer who is not preapproved may book up to 2 showings and is invited to a free meeting with an agent first.

RULES
No showings on Sundays. Pets stay out of the homes. Shoes off where the owner asks. Photos inside a home only with the owner's permission. Offers on a home go through an agent, never through the website.

CANCELLATIONS
A buyer cancels or moves a showing at least 2 hours ahead. A buyer who misses two showings without notice books only through an agent.

DECISIONS BY THE BROKER
Elena Brooks, the managing broker, decides every exception to these rules, such as a Sunday showing, a showing after the last start time, or more than 4 showings in a day.
