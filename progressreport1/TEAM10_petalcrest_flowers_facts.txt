PETALCREST FLOWERS, FACT SHEET
Petalcrest Flowers is a made up florist in Radford, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real shop is accidental.

HOURS
Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm. Saturday, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm. Closed Sunday. Phone: 540-555-0210. Email: orders@petalcrest.example

EVERYDAY ARRANGEMENTS
Small vase arrangement, 45 dollars. Medium vase arrangement, 70 dollars. Large vase arrangement, 110 dollars. A dozen roses, 85 dollars. Potted orchid, 55 dollars. Sympathy arrangement, from 120 dollars.
Every arrangement uses seasonal flowers, so the colors and flowers may differ from the photos.

ORDERING AND DELIVERY
Orders placed by 12:00 pm are delivered the same day, Monday to Saturday. Delivery is 12 dollars in Radford, Christiansburg and Fairlawn and 20 dollars in Blacksburg and Pulaski. The shop does not deliver to hospitals' intensive care units, which do not allow flowers. A card message is free.

HOLIDAYS
For Valentine's Day and Mother's Day, orders close 3 days before the holiday, and the shop delivers on the holiday and the day before only. Holiday prices are 20 percent higher.

WEDDINGS AND EVENTS
Wedding and event flowers start at 1,200 dollars. A couple meets the designer at least 8 weeks before the date, and a 50 percent deposit holds the date. The deposit is refunded only if the event is cancelled 30 days or more ahead.

CHANGES AND REFUNDS
An everyday order can be changed or cancelled for a full refund until 10:00 am on the delivery day. Flowers are perishable, so a delivered order is not refunded, but the shop replaces an arrangement that wilts within 3 days.

DECISIONS BY THE OWNER
Grace Okafor, the owner, decides every exception to these rules, such as a same day order after 12:00 pm, a holiday order after the cutoff, or a wedding on less than 8 weeks' notice.
