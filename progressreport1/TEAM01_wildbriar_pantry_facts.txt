WILDBRIAR COMMUNITY PANTRY, FACT SHEET
Wildbriar Community Pantry is a made up food pantry in Christiansburg, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real organization is accidental.

HOURS
Pickups run Tuesday and Thursday from 3:00 pm to 7:00 pm and Saturday from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm, in 20 minute slots. Donations are accepted Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, at the side door.
Phone: 540-555-0111. Email: help@wildbriar.example

WHO MAY PICK UP
Any household living in Montgomery County may pick up groceries once a week. A household registers once with an address in the county and the number of people in the home. A first pickup needs a photo ID; later pickups need only the household's name.

WHAT A HOUSEHOLD RECEIVES
One box of shelf stable food for a household of 1 to 3 people, two boxes for 4 to 6 people, three boxes for 7 or more. Fresh produce and bread when available. Diapers and baby formula for households with a child under 2, by request at booking.

BOOKING A PICKUP
A household books a slot at least one day ahead. Each slot serves four households. A household that misses its slot may book a new one for the next pickup day.

DONATIONS
The pantry accepts unopened, unexpired shelf stable food, fresh produce from local gardens on the day it is picked, and money. It does not accept home cooked food, opened packages, alcohol or expired items. A donor who gives food or money receives a written receipt for their taxes, listing the items or the amount and the date. The pantry does not put a dollar value on donated food.

VOLUNTEERS
Volunteers work 3 hour shifts and must be 16 or older. Volunteers under 18 need a signed permission form from a parent.

DECISIONS BY THE DIRECTOR
Denise Holloway, the pantry director, decides every exception to these rules, such as a second pickup in one week, a household from outside the county, or an emergency pickup on a day the pantry is closed.
