HOLLOWMERE FARM, PUMPKINS AND CHRISTMAS TREES, FACT SHEET
Hollowmere Farm is a made up family farm near Floyd, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real farm is accidental.

SEASONS AND HOURS
Pumpkin patch: the last Saturday in September to October 31, Friday 2:00 pm to 6:00 pm, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 am to 6:00 pm.
Christmas trees: the Friday after Thanksgiving to December 22, Thursday and Friday 1:00 pm to 5:00 pm, Saturday and Sunday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
The farm is closed in heavy rain, snow or ice, and posts a closing by 7:00 am.
Phone: 540-555-0199. Email: visit@hollowmere.example

PRICES
Pumpkin patch admission: 8 dollars per person aged 3 and over, which includes the hayride and the corn maze. Pumpkins are 0.75 dollars per pound.
Christmas trees: Fraser fir, choose and cut, 70 dollars for a tree up to 7 feet, 95 dollars up to 9 feet. Precut trees, 60 dollars. Wreaths, 30 dollars. Shaking, baling and loading are free.

VISIT TIMES
On weekends a visit is reserved for a time slot of one hour, up to 120 visitors per slot. Weekday visits need no reservation. A group of 20 or more, such as a school class, books a weekday visit at least 7 days ahead and pays 6 dollars per person.

TREE RESERVATIONS
A customer may tag a tree in the field from November 1 to the Friday after Thanksgiving with a 25 dollar deposit, and cut it on any open day. The deposit counts toward the price and is not refunded.

RULES
Dogs on a leash are welcome in the pumpkin patch, not on the hayride. Only farm staff use the saws for trees over 9 feet. Children under 12 stay with an adult.

DECISIONS BY THE MANAGER
June Caldwell, the farm manager, decides every exception to these rules, such as a weekend slot above capacity, a group visit on less than 7 days' notice, or a refund of a tree deposit.
