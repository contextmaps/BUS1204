MAROON HOLLOW GOODS, ONLINE SHOP, FACT SHEET
Maroon Hollow Goods is a made up online shop in Blacksburg, Virginia, that sells Virginia Tech themed apparel and gifts, used for a class at Virginia Tech. The shop and its products are made up and are not licensed by any university. Any resemblance to a real business is accidental.

CUSTOMER SERVICE
Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. Phone: 540-555-0244. Email: help@maroonhollow.example
The warehouse packs and ships orders Monday to Friday.

PRODUCTS
Game day t-shirt, 24 dollars, sizes S to 3XL. Hooded sweatshirt, 48 dollars, sizes S to 3XL. Knit beanie, 20 dollars. Ceramic mug, 16 dollars. Stadium blanket, 55 dollars. Name and number on a shirt or sweatshirt, 12 dollars extra.

SHIPPING IN THE UNITED STATES
Standard, 6 dollars, delivered in 4 to 7 business days. Free on orders of 75 dollars or more. Express, 18 dollars, delivered in 2 business days. Orders placed by 1:00 pm on a weekday ship the same day.

SHIPPING ABROAD
To Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan and South Korea only, 25 dollars, delivered in 7 to 14 business days. Prices abroad are shown in the customer's currency and charged in dollars. The customer pays any import tax on delivery.

PERSONALIZED ITEMS
A shirt or sweatshirt with a name and number ships in 5 business days. A personalized item cannot be returned or exchanged, unless it arrives damaged or wrong.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES
Items that are not personalized may be returned unworn, with tags, within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Return shipping is free in the United States and paid by the customer abroad. An exchange for another size ships when the return arrives.

ORDER CHANGES
An order can be changed or cancelled until it ships. After it ships, the customer returns it.

DECISIONS BY THE MANAGER
Rachel Kim, the operations manager, decides every exception to these rules, such as a return after 30 days, a refund of shipping, or a return of a personalized item.
