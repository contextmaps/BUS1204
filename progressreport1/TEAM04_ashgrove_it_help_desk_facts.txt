ASHGROVE ACCOUNTING GROUP, IT HELP DESK, FACT SHEET
Ashgrove Accounting Group is a made up accounting company with 40 employees in Christiansburg, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Its IT help desk serves only Ashgrove employees. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental.

SERVICE HOURS
Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:30 pm. During tax season, February 1 to April 15, Saturday 9:00 am to 1:00 pm as well. Outside these hours an urgent problem goes to the on call phone, 540-555-0144.
Email: helpdesk@ashgrove.example

PRIORITY LEVELS
Urgent: a whole department cannot work, a suspected virus or phishing attack, or a client deadline today. Response within 30 minutes.
High: one employee cannot work. Response within 2 hours.
Normal: something is slow or inconvenient. Response within 1 business day.
Request: new equipment, software or access. Response within 3 business days.

TRY THESE FIRST
Printer jam or offline: turn the printer off for 30 seconds and back on.
Wi-Fi drops: forget the network AshgroveStaff and rejoin it.
Forgotten password: use the reset link on the sign in page. The help desk cannot read a password to an employee.
Outlook will not open: restart the computer before opening a ticket.

RULES
The help desk never asks for a password and never sends a link to reset one by email. An employee who receives such a message reports it as urgent and does not click it.
Software is installed only from the approved list. A request for other software needs the IT manager's approval.
New equipment over 300 dollars needs the employee's department head and the IT manager.
Client files stay on company computers. The help desk does not set up client files on a personal device.
An employee who loses a laptop or phone with company email reports it within 1 hour.

DECISIONS BY THE IT MANAGER
Priya Natarajan, the IT manager, decides every exception to these rules, such as software outside the approved list, a personal device for work, or moving a ticket ahead of its priority level.
