SPOKELINE CYCLES, RENTALS AND REPAIRS, FACT SHEET
Spokeline Cycles is a made up bike shop near the Virginia Tech campus in Blacksburg, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real shop is accidental.

HOURS
Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 6:00 pm. Sunday, 12:00 pm to 5:00 pm. Closed Monday. Phone: 540-555-0255. Email: shop@spokeline.example

RENTALS
City bike, 30 dollars a day. Mountain bike, 55 dollars a day, front suspension, for the Huckleberry Trail and Pandapas Pond. Electric bike, 70 dollars a day. Child's bike, 20 dollars a day. A helmet and lock are included.
A rental day runs from pickup to closing time. A weekend rental, Saturday pickup and Sunday return, costs one and a half days.
A renter must be 18 or older with a photo ID and a credit card. A renter aged 14 to 17 needs a parent present to sign. Electric bikes go only to renters 18 and older.
A rental reservation holds a bike until 1 hour after opening on the rental day.

REPAIRS
Flat tire repair, 15 dollars. Brake adjustment, 20 dollars. Basic tune up, 65 dollars. Full overhaul, 160 dollars. Parts are extra.
A customer books a drop off time. A tune up is ready in 3 business days and an overhaul in 7. The shop calls when a bike is ready and stores it free for 14 days, then charges 5 dollars a day.
The shop does not work on electric bike motors or batteries.

CANCELLATIONS AND DAMAGE
A rental cancelled 24 hours or more ahead is free. A later cancellation costs one day's rental. A rental bike returned with damage is charged for parts and labor. A rental returned late is charged another day.

WEATHER
The shop does not rent mountain bikes when the trails are closed after heavy rain, and offers a city bike or a full refund instead.

DECISIONS BY THE OWNER
Dana Whitfield, the shop owner, decides every exception to these rules, such as a rental to a renter under 18 without a parent, a refund of a late cancellation, or a repair moved ahead of the queue.
