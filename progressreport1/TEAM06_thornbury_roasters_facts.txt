THORNBURY COFFEE ROASTERS, CAFE ACCOUNTS, FACT SHEET
Thornbury Coffee Roasters is a made up coffee roaster in Radford, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. It sells roasted beans wholesale to cafes and restaurants. Any resemblance to a real business is accidental.

HOURS
Office: Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 3:00 pm. Phone: 540-555-0166. Email: wholesale@thornbury.example

THE COFFEES
Mill Street Blend, medium roast, Brazil and Colombia, 11.50 dollars per pound.
River Bend Espresso, dark roast, Brazil, 12.00 dollars per pound.
Highland Decaf, medium roast, Colombia, 13.00 dollars per pound.
Single origin of the month, light roast, 15.00 dollars per pound, limited to 20 pounds per account.
Beans come in 5 pound bags only, whole bean or ground for drip.

ACCOUNTS AND ORDERS
A cafe or restaurant opens an account with a business license and a delivery address. The minimum order is 20 pounds. Orders for the week close Monday at 12:00 pm. Coffee is roasted on Tuesday and delivered Wednesday in Blacksburg, Christiansburg and Radford, and Thursday in Floyd and Pulaski. Delivery is free; an order under 20 pounds is not delivered.

PAYMENT
Accounts pay within 15 days of delivery. An account more than 30 days past due is placed on hold, and no new order ships until it is paid.

CHANGES AND RETURNS
An order may be changed until Monday at 12:00 pm. Roasted coffee is not returned, because it cannot be resold. A bag that arrives damaged or stale is replaced at the next delivery when the account reports it within 2 days.

SAMPLES
A new account may request one free sample of up to 2 pounds.

DECISIONS BY THE OWNER
Hannah Whitlock, the owner, decides every exception to these rules, such as an order after the Monday cutoff, an order under 20 pounds, an extra delivery day, or an order from an account on hold.
