CARDAMOM CORNER MARKET, FACT SHEET
Cardamom Corner Market is a made up international grocery in Christiansburg, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real store is accidental.

HOURS
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 8:00 pm. Sunday, 11:00 am to 6:00 pm. Phone: 540-555-0188. Email: orders@cardamomcorner.example

WHAT THE STORE CARRIES
Foods from Latin America, East and South Asia, and the Middle East: rice, beans and lentils, spices, flours, sauces, frozen foods, fresh tortillas, fresh herbs and halal meat. A halal butcher counter is open Thursday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 6:00 pm.

SPECIAL ORDERS
A customer may special order an item the store does not stock, from one of the store's four suppliers: one in Mexico, one in India, one in South Korea and one in Lebanon. Orders go to the suppliers every other Monday, and items arrive 2 to 3 weeks later. Frozen and fresh items cannot be special ordered, only shelf stable foods.
A special order holds up to 10 units of one item and needs a 20 percent deposit. The store quotes a price in dollars when the order is placed, and the price is final once the deposit is paid. The store calls the customer when the order arrives, and holds it for 7 days.

CANCELLATIONS AND RETURNS
A special order can be cancelled for a full refund before the supplier order goes out. After that, the deposit is kept. Groceries from the shelves may be returned within 7 days with a receipt, except fresh herbs, meat and frozen foods.

BULK PURCHASES
Restaurants and households buying a full case receive 10 percent off the shelf price.

DECISIONS BY THE OWNER
Yusuf Haddad, the owner, decides every exception to these rules, such as a special order of more than 10 units, a fresh or frozen special order, or a refund of a deposit after the supplier order went out.
