LANTERN LOFT COWORKING, FACT SHEET
Lantern Loft Coworking is a made up coworking space in downtown Blacksburg, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real business is accidental.

HOURS
Members with a key card: every day, 6:00 am to 11:00 pm. Staffed front desk: Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. The space is closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Phone: 540-555-0221. Email: hello@lanternloft.example

MEMBERSHIPS
Day pass: 25 dollars, no key card, front desk hours only.
Flex desk: 175 dollars a month, any open desk, 4 meeting room hours a month.
Dedicated desk: 300 dollars a month, a desk of your own, 8 meeting room hours a month.
Private office for 2 to 4 people: 750 dollars a month, 15 meeting room hours a month.
Memberships run month to month and renew on the first of each month. A member cancels by the 20th to stop the next month's charge.

MEETING ROOMS
The Ridge room seats 4, 20 dollars an hour. The Valley room seats 10 and has a screen, 40 dollars an hour. Members use their monthly hours first. Rooms are booked in hours, from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, up to 30 days ahead, and up to 4 hours a day per member. A booking can be cancelled for free up to 24 hours ahead.

HOUSE RULES
Quiet zone on the second floor. Phone calls in the call booths. Guests sign in at the front desk and may stay up to 3 hours without a day pass. No pets except service animals. Food is fine; no cooking.

UNPAID ACCOUNTS
A member whose payment is more than 10 days late loses key card access until the account is paid.

DECISIONS BY THE MANAGER
Owen Stratton, the manager, decides every exception to these rules, such as a room booked for more than 4 hours, a booking more than 30 days ahead, or key card access on an unpaid account.
