LARKSPUR STUDY ABROAD, FACT SHEET
Larkspur Study Abroad is a made up travel agency in Blacksburg, Virginia, that arranges trips for college students, used for a class at Virginia Tech. The programs below are made up. Any resemblance to a real agency is accidental.

OFFICE HOURS
Monday to Friday, 10:00 am to 5:00 pm. Phone: 540-555-0177. Email: advisers@larkspur.example

PROGRAMS
Spring break in Costa Rica: 8 days, March, 2,450 dollars. Ecology and service work.
Summer in Seville, Spain: 4 weeks, June, 5,900 dollars. Spanish language classes, homestay.
Summer in Kyoto, Japan: 3 weeks, July, 6,800 dollars. Culture and business visits.
Fall semester in Prague, Czech Republic: 14 weeks, 13,500 dollars. Courses for transfer credit.
Prices include housing, ground transport, program activities and travel insurance. Prices do not include airfare, meals other than breakfast, passports or visas. Prices are quoted in dollars and may change until a deposit is paid, because the agency pays hotels and guides in local currency.

WHO MAY GO
Students aged 18 or older, enrolled at a college or university, with a grade point average of 2.5 or higher. Every student needs a passport valid for 6 months after the return date. Kyoto requires one semester of any language course; Seville requires one semester of Spanish.

APPLYING
A student sends an inquiry, meets with an adviser, then applies. Applications close 90 days before departure. A 500 dollar deposit holds a place, and the balance is due 60 days before departure.

CANCELLATIONS
A student who cancels more than 60 days before departure receives the payments back except the 500 dollar deposit. Between 60 and 30 days, the agency keeps half of the program price. Less than 30 days, no refund. Travel insurance covers cancellation for medical reasons with a doctor's note.

DECISIONS BY THE DIRECTOR
Dr. Naomi Adeyemi, the director, decides every exception to these rules, such as an application after the deadline, a grade point average below 2.5, or a refund outside the cancellation policy.
