IVYHAVEN PROPERTY MANAGEMENT, MAINTENANCE, FACT SHEET
Ivyhaven Property Management is a made up company that runs three apartment buildings near the Virginia Tech campus in Blacksburg, used for a class at Virginia Tech: Linden Court, Ashby Place and Carrow Hall. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental.

OFFICE HOURS
Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. Phone: 540-555-0232. Email: maintenance@ivyhaven.example
Emergency line, every hour of every day: 540-555-0233.

WHAT COUNTS AS AN EMERGENCY
Fire, smoke or a gas smell: leave the building and call 911 first. Water flowing from a pipe or ceiling. No heat when the outside temperature is below 40 degrees. No working toilet in the apartment. A broken exterior door or window lock. Sewage backup. A technician arrives within 2 hours.

URGENT
No hot water. A refrigerator that stops cooling. A stove that does not work. A leak that is dripping, not flowing. A technician arrives within 1 business day.

ROUTINE
Everything else, such as a dripping faucet, a running toilet, a stuck drawer or a burned out light fixture. A technician arrives within 5 business days.

ENTERING AN APARTMENT
For a routine or urgent request, the tenant chooses whether a technician may enter while the apartment is empty. For an emergency, a technician may enter at once. Technicians work Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, except for emergencies.

WHAT TENANTS FIX THEMSELVES
Light bulbs, batteries in smoke detectors, and a clogged sink drain that a plunger clears.

CHARGES
Repairs are free, except damage a tenant or guest caused, such as a broken window or an object flushed down a toilet. Such repairs are charged at cost, and the tenant is told the cost before work starts.

DECISIONS BY THE MANAGER
Calvin Mercer, the maintenance manager, decides every exception to these rules, such as a routine repair moved ahead of others, work on a weekend that is not an emergency, or waiving a charge for damage.
