COPPER LADLE FOOD TRUCK, FACT SHEET
The Copper Ladle is a made up food truck in Blacksburg, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real business is accidental.

SCHEDULE
Monday: Drillfield, 11:00 am to 2:00 pm. Tuesday: Corporate Research Center, 11:00 am to 2:00 pm. Wednesday: Downtown Blacksburg, 11:00 am to 2:00 pm. Thursday: Christiansburg Huckleberry Park, 5:00 pm to 8:00 pm. Friday: Downtown Blacksburg, 11:00 am to 2:00 pm and 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm. Saturday and Sunday: closed, except for booked events.
Phone: 540-555-0133. Email: orders@copperladle.example

MENU
Smoked chicken bowl, 12 dollars. Black bean bowl (vegetarian), 11 dollars. Pulled pork sandwich, 11 dollars. Grilled cheese with tomato soup, 9 dollars. Side of street corn, 4 dollars. Lemonade, 3 dollars. The black bean bowl can be made vegan on request. The kitchen uses peanuts in the street corn topping, so the truck cannot promise any item is free of peanuts.

PREORDERS
A customer preorders for pickup at that day's stop, no later than 9:00 am the same day for lunch and 2:00 pm for dinner. A preorder may hold up to 10 meals. A preorder is paid at the window at pickup. A preorder not picked up within 30 minutes of the end of service is given away.

CATERING AND EVENTS
The truck caters events of 25 to 150 people on weekends, at 14 dollars per person, booked at least 14 days ahead with a 100 dollar deposit. The deposit is refunded if the event is cancelled 7 days or more ahead.

WEATHER
The truck does not open for a stop when thunderstorms or snow are forecast during service. The owner posts the change by 8:00 am, and preorders for that stop are cancelled.

DECISIONS BY THE OWNER
Tomas Reyes, the owner, decides every exception to these rules, such as a preorder after the cutoff, more than 10 meals, or catering on less than 14 days' notice.
