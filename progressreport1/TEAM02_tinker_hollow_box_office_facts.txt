TINKER HOLLOW PLAYERS, BOX OFFICE, FACT SHEET
Tinker Hollow Players is a made up community theater in Blacksburg, Virginia, used for a class at Virginia Tech. Any resemblance to a real theater is accidental.

THE SEASON
Four productions a year, each running two weekends. Performances are Friday and Saturday at 7:30 pm and Sunday at 2:00 pm. The theater seats 180: 120 seats in the orchestra and 60 in the balcony. The balcony has no elevator.
Box office: 540-555-0122, open Wednesday to Friday, 12:00 pm to 6:00 pm, and one hour before every performance. Email: tickets@tinkerhollow.example

PRICES
Orchestra: 24 dollars for adults, 18 dollars for students and seniors aged 65 and over, 12 dollars for children under 12. Balcony: 16 dollars for every seat. A group of 10 or more pays 20 dollars per orchestra seat.

HOLDS
A customer may hold up to 8 seats for one performance. A hold lasts 48 hours. The customer pays at the box office or by phone before the hold ends, or the seats return to sale. Holds close 24 hours before the performance.

WAITLIST
When a performance sells out, customers join a waitlist. When seats return to sale, the box office offers them to the waitlist in order.

ACCESSIBILITY
Four wheelchair spaces in the first orchestra row, each with a companion seat at the same price. Assisted listening devices are free at the box office.

REFUNDS AND EXCHANGES
Tickets are not refunded. A ticket may be exchanged for another performance of the same production up to 24 hours before the original performance, for a fee of 3 dollars per ticket. If the theater cancels a performance, every customer receives a full refund or an exchange.

RULES
Children under 5 are not admitted except to the family matinee of each production. Latecomers are seated at intermission.

DECISIONS BY THE MANAGER
Marcus Ellery, the box office manager, decides every exception to these rules, such as a hold longer than 48 hours, more than 8 seats in one hold, or an exchange inside 24 hours.
