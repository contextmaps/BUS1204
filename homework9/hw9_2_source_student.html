A Produce Distributor's Rules in an Agent, a Skill and a Price Sheet
Platform: Hokie AI · Submission: One regular Hokie AI chat holding both checks and both reflections, exported as a .zip and uploaded to Canvas
Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, students will be able to:

  1. Work out a correct order confirmation from a price sheet and an agent's instructions before the reply arrives.
  2. Run the same order through a shared agent with and without a company skill, and trace every difference to the price sheet, the skill or the agent's instructions.
  3. Place a business's rules in an agent's instructions, a company skill, an employee's own skill or a price sheet, with a reason for every rule.
  4. Move a misplaced rule out of a skill, rerun the order, and show the effect with the numbers from both runs.

The Mossgrove Order Desk, its instructions
You are the order desk of Mossgrove Produce, a made up produce distributor in Roanoke, Virginia that delivers fresh produce to restaurants. When a restaurant sends an order, reply with a confirmation of that order.

Price every item from the price sheet in your knowledge. Give each item's amount and the order's subtotal, and name the date of the price sheet you used.

If the subtotal is under 150.00 dollars, add a delivery fee of 25.00 dollars on its own line and then give the total. If the subtotal is 150.00 dollars or more, write Delivery fee: none, and then give the total.

Mossgrove delivers on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday only. If an order asks for any other day, offer the next delivery day after it and do not confirm the day the customer asked for.

Never state a delivery time or a time window. Say that the driver confirms the delivery window by 6 pm the day before.

Sign every confirmation Mossgrove Produce, Order Desk. Never sign with a person's name.
The agent's whole instructions. Its one knowledge source is the price sheet below.

Class material
The owner's memo
From: Renata Calloway, owner, Mossgrove Produce
To: the order desk team
About: where our rules live

We now have an order desk agent, a confirmation skill that every one of you has installed, and a price sheet. I want each of our rules to live in one place only. Here are six of them.

1. Romaine goes up to 41.00 dollars a case on Monday, November 2.
2. Every confirmation lists one item per line, with its amount.
3. Orders with a subtotal under 150.00 dollars pay a 25.00 dollar delivery fee.
4. We never tell a customer a delivery time. The driver confirms the window by 6 pm the day before.
5. Our sales reps may sign emails to their regular chefs with their own first name.
6. Fresh basil is sold by the pound, and the smallest order is half a pound.
Six rules. Each one gets a place and a reason in your log.
