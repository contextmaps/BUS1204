CARDAMOM CORNER MARKET, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM08. Cardamom Corner Market is made up, and so are the items, suppliers, customers and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Cardamom Corner Market is an international grocery in Christiansburg. Customers special order shelf stable items the store does not stock, from its suppliers in Mexico, India, South Korea and Lebanon, with a 20 percent deposit, and may cancel before the supplier order goes out. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a special order, and a cancellation of a special order. Your staff agent works with Yusuf Haddad, the owner, who decides every request. The store pays its suppliers in Mexican pesos, Indian rupees and Korean won, so your staff agent checks the exchange rates, and the owner uses them to set the dollar price of each special order item.

The system has five parts:
Items tab: the items customers may special order, their supplier prices and their dollar prices. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the owner's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Items tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Items tab and the exchange rates, and records the owner's decision after the owner confirms.
Yusuf Haddad: decides every request and updates the Items tab by hand.

7. THE OWNER'S DECISIONS

Yusuf Haddad decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Items tab say, and the owner decides.

A special order. The staff agent checks that the item is orderable, that the order holds 10 units or fewer, and works out the total and the 20 percent deposit from quoted_usd. The owner marks the request ordered or declines it. More than 10 units, or a fresh or frozen item, is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note. The price is final once the customer pays the deposit.

A cancellation. The staff agent finds the special order by its request_id and checks whether the supplier order has gone out (orders go to the suppliers every other Monday). Before it goes out, the customer receives the full deposit back. The owner marks the cancellation refunded or declines it. A refund of the deposit after the supplier order went out is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.

The exchange rates. When the owner asks about the rates, the staff agent calls get_rates, reports how many pesos, rupees and won one dollar buys today, and works out each item's supplier price in dollars. The owner sets price_usd on the Items tab by hand: the supplier price in dollars plus 40 percent, rounded to the nearest 50 cents. Orders already placed keep their quoted price.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_items before answering any question about special order items or their prices, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a special order, collect the customer's name, phone number, the item, the number of units, and the price_usd of one unit. For a cancellation, collect the name, phone number and the request_id of the order. Repeat the details back.
When the customer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the store confirms every order and calls when it arrives. Never say that an item is ordered or a deposit refunded.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the customer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the owner asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_items when the owner asks about an item, and get_rates for the exchange rates.
Record a decision only when the owner names a request_id and says order, refund or decline. Repeat it, ask the owner to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After the owner asks about the rates, remind the owner which prices on the Items tab may need updating.
