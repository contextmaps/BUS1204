SPOKELINE CYCLES, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM14. Spokeline Cycles is made up, and so are the customers and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Spokeline Cycles is a bike shop in Blacksburg that rents bikes by the day and repairs bikes that customers drop off. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a rental, and a repair drop off. Your staff agent works with Dana Whitfield, the shop owner, who decides every request. Customers rent more bikes on warm, dry weekends, and the shop does not rent mountain bikes when the trails close after heavy rain, so your staff agent checks the weekend forecast, and the owner uses it to decide how many bikes to prepare and whether mountain bikes go out.

The system has five parts:
Rentals tab: the rental days of the coming weekend, bike type by bike type, with the bikes left. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the owner's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Rentals tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Rentals tab and the forecast, and records the owner's decision after the owner confirms.
Dana Whitfield: decides every request and updates the Rentals tab by hand.

7. THE OWNER'S DECISIONS

Dana Whitfield decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Rentals tab say, and the owner decides.

A rental. The staff agent checks that the day and bike type are open and have enough bikes left, that the renter is 18 or older, or 14 to 17 with a parent present to sign, and that electric bikes go only to renters 18 and older. It works out the price, with a weekend rental from Saturday to Sunday at one and a half days. The owner reserves or declines. A rental to a renter under 18 without a parent is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.
After a reserved rental: the owner lowers bikes_left for that day and type by the number of bikes on the Rentals tab, and writes full in status when it reaches 0.

A repair. The staff agent checks the service and its price, that the shop is open at the drop off time (Tuesday to Saturday 10:00 am to 6:00 pm, Sunday 12:00 pm to 5:00 pm), that the bike is not an electric bike needing motor or battery work, and gives the ready time (3 business days for a tune up, 7 for an overhaul). The owner books or declines. A repair moved ahead of the queue is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.

A rainy weekend. When the owner asks about the weather, the staff agent calls get_forecast and reports the chance of rain, the rain in inches and the high temperature for each day. The forecast covers the next 16 days, so the owner looks at the coming weekend. On a warm, dry weekend the owner prepares more bikes and schedules more employees. After heavy rain, when the trails close, the owner writes closed in the status of the mountain bike rows on the Rentals tab by hand, and the staff agent drafts a message offering a city bike or a full refund to the customers with a reserved mountain bike.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_rentals before answering any question about rental bikes, prices or bikes left, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a rental, collect the customer's name, the day and bike type, the number of bikes, the renter's age group and a phone number. For a repair, collect the name, the service, the drop off date and time and a phone number. Repeat the details back.
When the customer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the shop confirms every request. Never say that a bike is reserved.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the customer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the owner asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_rentals when the owner asks whether a rental fits, and get_forecast for the weather.
Record a decision only when the owner names a request_id and says reserve, book or decline. Repeat it, ask the owner to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a reserved rental, remind the owner to lower bikes_left on the Rentals tab.
