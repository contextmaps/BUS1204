THORNBURY COFFEE ROASTERS, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM06. Thornbury Coffee Roasters is made up, and so are the cafes, accounts and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Thornbury Coffee Roasters roasts coffee in Radford and sells it wholesale to cafes and restaurants. Accounts order by Monday at 12:00 pm, the coffee is roasted on Tuesday and delivered on Wednesday or Thursday, and a new account may ask for a free sample. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a weekly order, and a sample. Your staff agent works with Hannah Whitlock, the owner, who decides every request. The roaster buys its green beans from Brazil, so your staff agent checks the exchange rate for the Brazilian real, and the owner uses it to decide when to buy beans and whether to change wholesale prices.

The system has five parts:
Coffees tab: the coffees, their wholesale prices and how many pounds are left in this week's roast. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the owner's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Coffees tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It holds no office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Coffees tab and the exchange rate, and records the owner's decision after the owner confirms.
Hannah Whitlock: decides every request and updates the Coffees tab by hand.

7. THE OWNER'S DECISIONS

Hannah Whitlock decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Coffees tab say, and the owner decides.

An order. The staff agent checks that the coffee has enough pounds left, that the pounds come in 5 pound bags, that the account's order for the week reaches the 20 pound minimum across its requests, that the single origin stays within 20 pounds per account, and that the order came in by Monday at 12:00 pm. The owner confirms or declines. An order after the Monday cutoff, an order under 20 pounds, an extra delivery day, or an order from an account on hold is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.
After a confirmed order: the owner lowers pounds_left for that coffee by the pounds ordered on the Coffees tab, and writes sold out in status when it reaches 0.

A sample. The staff agent checks that the account is new and asks for up to 2 pounds. The owner marks it sent or declines it.
After a sent sample: the owner lowers pounds_left by the sample's pounds.

The exchange rate. When the owner asks about the real, the staff agent calls get_rates and reports how many reais one dollar buys today. When one dollar buys more reais than at the last purchase, green beans from Brazil cost less in dollars, and the owner may buy more beans now. When it buys fewer, the owner may raise price_per_pound for the Brazilian coffees on the Coffees tab, by hand, for the next week's orders.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:

After a confirmed order or a sent sample, remind the owner to lower pounds_left on the Coffees tab.
