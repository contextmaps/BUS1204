LANTERN LOFT COWORKING, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM11. Lantern Loft Coworking is made up, and so are the members, companies and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Lantern Loft Coworking is a coworking space in Blacksburg with desks, private offices and two meeting rooms, the Ridge room for 4 and the Valley room for 10. Members book meeting rooms in hours, and memberships run month to month. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a meeting room booking, and a membership change, which starts or cancels a membership. Your staff agent works with Owen Stratton, the manager, who decides every request. The space closes on public holidays, so your staff agent checks the public holiday calendar, and the manager uses it to block bookings on those days and to adjust monthly fees.

The system has five parts:
Rooms tab: the meeting room times of the week and whether each is open. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the manager's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Rooms tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It holds no office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Rooms tab and the holiday calendar, and records the manager's decision after the manager confirms.
Owen Stratton: decides every request and updates the Rooms tab by hand.

Parameter Schema, COPY: {"type":"object","properties":{}}

list_requests

manager_note   The manager's note. When the manager gives no note, the agent sends the word none.
required       The agent must send all five values, so every decision has a note.


7. THE MANAGER'S DECISIONS

Owen Stratton decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Rooms tab say, and the manager decides.

A room. The staff agent checks that the time is open, that the room seats the people (Ridge 4, Valley 10), that the booking is at most 30 days ahead and keeps the member at 4 hours a day or fewer, and works out how many of the member's monthly hours it uses (flex desk 4, dedicated desk 8, private office 15) and the charge after them (Ridge 20 dollars an hour, Valley 40). A day pass holder has no monthly hours. The manager books or declines. A room for more than 4 hours, a booking more than 30 days ahead, or key card access on an unpaid account is an exception, and the manager decides it with a note.
After a booked room: the manager writes booked in that time's status on the Rooms tab.

A membership change. The staff agent checks the plan and its price, and for a cancellation whether it came by the 20th, which stops the next month's charge. The manager marks it updated or declines it.

A holiday. When the manager asks about holidays, the staff agent calls get_holidays and reports the holidays of the coming months. The space closes on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. The manager writes closed in the status of every room time on those days on the Rooms tab, by hand, and may adjust the monthly fees for a month with a closed day.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_rooms before answering any question about meeting rooms or room times, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a room, collect the member's name, plan, the room time, the number of people and an email. For a membership change, collect the name, the plan, start or cancel with the month, and an email. Repeat the details back.
When the member says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the front desk confirms every request. Never say that a room is booked.
The agent cannot see other requests or other members' accounts, and says it can help only with the member's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the manager asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_rooms when the manager asks whether a booking fits, and get_holidays for holidays and closing days.
Record a decision only when the manager names a request_id and says book, update or decline. Repeat it, ask the manager to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a booked room, remind the manager to write booked on the Rooms tab.
