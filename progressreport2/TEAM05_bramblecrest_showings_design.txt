1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Bramblecrest Realty is a real estate agency in Blacksburg with homes for sale in Blacksburg, Christiansburg and Riner. Buyers book 30 minute showings, and a Bramblecrest agent leads every showing. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a showing, and a change to a showing, which cancels or moves it. Your staff agent works with Elena Brooks, the managing broker, who decides every request. The last showing of the day starts one hour before sunset, and buyers avoid rain, so your staff agent checks the forecast with the sunset time, and the broker uses it to set the last start time of each day.

The system has five parts:
Showings tab: the showing times of the week, home by home, and whether each is open. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the broker's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Showings tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Showings tab and the forecast with the sunset time, and records the broker's decision after the broker confirms.
Elena Brooks: decides every request and updates the Showings tab by hand.

7. THE BROKER'S DECISIONS

Elena Brooks decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Showings tab say, and the broker decides.

A showing. The staff agent checks that the time is open, at least 24 hours away and not on a Sunday; that the home allows it (BR-103 only on weekdays after 4:00 pm and on weekends, and BR-105 is under contract); and that the buyer has at most 4 showings that day, or at most 2 without a preapproval. The broker confirms or declines. A Sunday showing, a showing after the last start time, or more than 4 in a day is an exception, and the broker decides it with a note.
After a confirmed showing: the broker writes booked in that time's status on the Showings tab.

A change. The staff agent finds the showing by its request_id or its time, and checks that the change comes at least 2 hours before the showing and, for a move, that the new time is open. The broker marks it changed or declines it.
After a changed request: the broker writes open in the old time's status, and for a move, booked in the new time's status.

The last start time. When the broker asks about the weather or the sunset, the staff agent calls get_forecast and reports the sunset, the last start time one hour before it, and the chance of rain for each day. The forecast covers the next 16 days, so the broker looks at the coming days. The broker writes closed in the status of any open time after the last start time, by hand.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_showings before answering any question about homes or showing times, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a showing, collect the buyer's name, the slot_id, a phone number, an email and whether the buyer is preapproved. For a change, collect the name, the phone number, the request_id or the time of the showing, cancel or move, and the new time for a move. Repeat the details back.
When the buyer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that Bramblecrest confirms every showing. Never say that a showing is booked.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the buyer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the broker asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_showings when the broker asks whether a showing fits, and get_forecast for the weather, the sunset and the last start time.
Record a decision only when the broker names a request_id and says confirm, change or decline. Repeat it, ask the broker to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a confirmed showing or a change, remind the broker to update the status on the Showings tab.
