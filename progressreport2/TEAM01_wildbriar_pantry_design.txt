WILDBRIAR COMMUNITY PANTRY, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM01. Wildbriar Community Pantry is made up, and so are the households, donors and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Wildbriar Community Pantry gives groceries to households in Montgomery County and accepts donations of food and money. Households book a 20 minute pickup slot, and each slot serves four households. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a household booking a pickup slot, and a donor offering a donation. Your staff agent works with Denise Holloway, the pantry director, who decides every request. Demand rises in cold weather, so your staff agent checks the forecast, and the director uses it to add pickup slots on cold days.

The system has five parts:
Slots tab: the pickup slots and how many households each can still take. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the director's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Slots tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Slots tab and the forecast, and records the director's decision after the director confirms.
Denise Holloway: decides every request and updates the Slots tab by hand.

7. THE DIRECTOR'S DECISIONS

Denise Holloway decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Slots tab say, and the director decides.

A pickup. The staff agent checks that the slot is open and has a household left, and that the slot is at least one day after the request. The director approves or declines, and checks with the household that baby items are for a child under 2. A household from outside Montgomery County, a second pickup in one week, or a pickup on a closed day is an exception, and the director decides it with a note.
After an approved pickup: the director lowers households_left for that slot by 1 on the Slots tab, and writes full in status when it reaches 0.

A donation. The staff agent checks the offer against the fact sheet: unopened, unexpired shelf stable food, garden produce picked that day, or money, and never home cooked food, opened packages, alcohol or expired items. The director accepts or declines.
After an accepted donation: the staff agent drafts the donor's receipt from progress report 1, listing the items or the amount and the date, with no dollar value for food.

A cold week. When the director asks about the weather, the staff agent calls get_forecast and reports the low temperature and the chance of snow for each day. The forecast covers the next 16 days, so the director looks at the coming pickup days. On a pickup day with a low below 25 degrees, the director may add a slot. The director adds the row to the Slots tab by hand, with a new slot_id such as WB-T05, households_left 4 and status open.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_slots before answering any question about pickup days, times or open slots, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a pickup, collect the household's name, the slot_id, the number of people and whether it needs baby items. For a donation, collect the donor's name and what the donor offers. Repeat the details back.
When the customer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the pantry confirms every request. Never say that a pickup is booked or a donation accepted.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the customer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the director asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_slots when the director asks whether a pickup fits, and get_forecast for the weather.
Record a decision only when the director names a request_id and says approve, accept or decline. Repeat it, ask the director to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After an approved pickup, remind the director to lower households_left on the Slots tab. After an accepted donation, draft the receipt.
