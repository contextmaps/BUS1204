LARKSPUR STUDY ABROAD, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM07. Larkspur Study Abroad is made up, and so are the students and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Larkspur Study Abroad is a travel agency in Blacksburg that runs four programs for college students, in Costa Rica, Seville, Kyoto and Prague. Students send an inquiry, meet an adviser and apply, and a student who has paid may cancel under the cancellation policy. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: an inquiry about a program, and a cancellation. Your staff agent works with Dr. Naomi Adeyemi, the director, who decides every request. The agency pays hotels and guides in euros, Japanese yen and Czech koruna, so your staff agent checks the exchange rates, and the director uses them to update the dollar prices of the programs.

The system has five parts:
Programs tab: the programs, their dollar prices, the places left and the application deadlines. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the director's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Programs tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Programs tab and the exchange rates, and records the director's decision after the director confirms.
Dr. Naomi Adeyemi: decides every request and updates the Programs tab by hand.

7. THE DIRECTOR'S DECISIONS

Dr. Naomi Adeyemi decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Programs tab say, and the director decides.

An inquiry. The staff agent checks that the program is open and has places left, that the deadline has not passed, that the grade point average is 2.5 or higher, and that the student has the language course the program needs (one semester of Spanish for Seville, one semester of any language for Kyoto). The director sets a meeting with an adviser, named with the time in the note, or declines. An application after the deadline or a grade point average below 2.5 is an exception, and the director decides it with a note.

A cancellation. The staff agent works out the refund from the policy: more than 60 days before departure, everything paid except the 500 dollar deposit; 60 to 30 days, half the program price kept; less than 30 days, no refund, unless travel insurance covers a medical reason with a doctor's note. The director marks the request refunded, with the amount in the note, or declines. A refund outside the policy is an exception.
After a refunded cancellation: the director raises places_left for that program by 1 on the Programs tab, and writes open in status if the program was full.

The exchange rates. When the director asks about the rates, the staff agent calls get_rates and reports how many euros, yen and koruna one dollar buys today. The Costa Rica program pays its partners in dollars, so its price stays as it is. When one dollar buys fewer euros, yen or koruna than before, the program in that currency costs more to run, and the director may raise its price_usd on the Programs tab, by hand. A student who has paid a deposit keeps the old price.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_programs before answering any question about programs, dates, prices, places or deadlines, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For an inquiry, collect the student's name, email, the program, the grade point average, any language course and the question. For a cancellation, collect the name, email, the program, how many days before departure, and the reason. Repeat the details back.
When the student says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that an adviser or the director replies to every request. Never promise a place, a price or a refund.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the student's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the director asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_programs when the director asks whether a student fits a program, and get_rates for the exchange rates.
Record a decision only when the director names a request_id and says meet, refund or decline. Repeat it, ask the director to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a refunded cancellation, remind the director to raise places_left on the Programs tab.
