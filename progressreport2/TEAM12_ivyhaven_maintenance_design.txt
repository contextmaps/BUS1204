TEAM12. Ivyhaven Property Management is made up, and so are the buildings, tenants and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Ivyhaven Property Management runs apartment buildings in Blacksburg. Tenants report maintenance problems, and technicians visit Monday to Friday, within 1 business day for an urgent problem and 5 for a routine one. An emergency goes to the emergency line, never to the agent. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a repair, and a change to the time of a repair visit. Your staff agent works with Calvin Mercer, the maintenance manager, who decides every request. A hard freeze can burst pipes, so your staff agent checks the freeze and storm forecast, and the manager uses it to move heating and plumbing repairs to the front of the queue.

The system has five parts:
Visits tab: the technicians' visit times of the week and whether each is open. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the maintenance manager's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Visits tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It holds no office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Visits tab and the freeze and storm forecast, and records the maintenance manager's decision after the manager confirms.
Calvin Mercer: decides every request and updates the Visits tab by hand.

7. THE MAINTENANCE MANAGER'S DECISIONS

Calvin Mercer decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Visits tab say, and the manager decides.

A repair. The staff agent checks the category against the fact sheet (urgent for no hot water, a refrigerator that stops cooling, a stove that does not work or a dripping leak; routine for everything else), that the problem is not one tenants fix themselves, such as a light bulb or a smoke detector battery, and that the visit time is open and falls within 1 business day for urgent or 5 for routine. If the problem sounds like an emergency, the staff agent says so at once, and the manager calls the tenant. The manager schedules or declines. A routine repair moved ahead of others, weekend work that is not an emergency, or waiving a charge for damage is an exception, and the manager decides it with a note.
After a scheduled repair: the manager writes booked in that visit's status on the Visits tab.

A reschedule. The staff agent finds the repair by its request_id or its current visit, and checks that the new time is open. The manager marks it moved or declines it.
After a moved visit: the manager writes open in the old time's status and booked in the new time's status.

A hard freeze. When the manager asks about the weather, the staff agent calls get_forecast and reports the low temperature, the wind gusts, and the rain and snow for each day. The forecast covers the next 16 days, so the manager looks at the coming days. When a low of 28 degrees or below is forecast, the manager moves heating and plumbing repairs to the earliest open visits, and marks the routine repairs that were in those times moved, with a note.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_visits before answering any question about when a technician can come, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For an emergency on the fact sheet, give the emergency line, 540-555-0233, and 911 first for fire, smoke or a gas smell, and never call add_request.
For a repair, collect the tenant's name, the apartment, the category, the problem, whether a technician may enter while the apartment is empty, the visit time and a phone number. For a reschedule, collect the name, apartment, the request_id or the current time of the repair, the new time and a phone number. Repeat the details back.
When the tenant says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the maintenance office confirms every visit. Never say that a visit is scheduled.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the tenant's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the manager asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_visits when the manager asks whether a visit time is open, and get_forecast for freezes and storms.
Record a decision only when the manager names a request_id and says schedule, move or decline. Repeat it, ask the manager to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a scheduled repair or a moved visit, remind the manager to update the Visits tab.
