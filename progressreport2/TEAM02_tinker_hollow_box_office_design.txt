TINKER HOLLOW PLAYERS, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM02. Tinker Hollow Players is made up, and so are the production, customers and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Tinker Hollow Players is a community theater in Blacksburg with 120 orchestra seats and 60 balcony seats. Customers hold seats for a performance and pay at the box office within 48 hours, and a sold out performance has a waitlist. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a hold on seats, and a place on the waitlist. Your staff agent works with Marcus Ellery, the box office manager, who decides every request. Attendance rises on holiday weekends, so your staff agent checks the public holiday calendar, and the manager uses it to decide whether to add a performance.

The system has five parts:
Performances tab: every performance of the current production and the seats left in the orchestra and the balcony. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the box office manager's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Performances tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It holds no office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Performances tab and the holiday calendar, and records the box office manager's decision after the manager confirms.
Marcus Ellery: decides every request and updates the Performances tab by hand.

list_requests

manager_note   The box office manager's note. When the box office manager gives no note, the agent sends the word none.
required       The agent must send all five values, so every decision has a note.


7. THE BOX OFFICE MANAGER'S DECISIONS

Marcus Ellery decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Performances tab say, and the manager decides.

A hold. The staff agent checks that the performance has enough seats left in the section, that the hold is for 8 seats or fewer, and that the performance is more than 24 hours away. A wheelchair space is in the first orchestra row, and the theater has four. The manager marks the request held or declined. More than 8 seats, a hold longer than 48 hours, or a hold inside 24 hours is an exception, and the manager decides it with a note.
After a held request: the manager lowers orchestra_left or balcony_left by the number of seats on the Performances tab, and writes sold out in status when both reach 0.

A waitlist place. The staff agent checks that the performance is sold out. The manager marks the request waitlisted or declined. When seats return to sale, the manager calls the waitlist in the order of the request_ids.

A holiday weekend. When the manager asks about holidays, the staff agent calls get_holidays and reports the holidays near the production's weekends. Presidents' Day falls on the Monday after the first weekend of this production. The manager may add a performance on a holiday weekend, by hand, as a new row in the Performances tab with a new perf_id such as TH-07, 120 orchestra seats, 60 balcony seats and status open.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_performances before answering any question about performances, times or seats, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a hold, collect the customer's name, the perf_id, the section, the number of seats, whether a wheelchair space is needed, and a phone number. For a sold out performance, offer the waitlist and collect the same details. Repeat the details back.
When the customer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the box office confirms every hold. Never say that seats are held or sold.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the customer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the manager asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_performances when the manager asks whether a hold fits, and get_holidays for holiday weekends.
Record a decision only when the manager names a request_id and says hold, waitlist or decline. Repeat it, ask the manager to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a held request, remind the manager to lower the seats left on the Performances tab.
