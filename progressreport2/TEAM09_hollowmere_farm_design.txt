HOLLOWMERE FARM, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM09. Hollowmere Farm is made up, and so are the visitors, groups and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Hollowmere Farm in Floyd runs a pumpkin patch in the fall and sells Christmas trees in December. On weekends, visitors reserve a one hour time slot, up to 120 visitors per slot, and groups of 20 or more book a weekday visit at least 7 days ahead. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a weekend visit, and a group visit. Your staff agent works with June Caldwell, the farm manager, who decides every request. Visits rise on clear weekends and the farm closes in heavy rain, snow or ice, so your staff agent checks the weekend forecast, and the manager uses it to schedule staff and to close a day.

The system has five parts:
Slots tab: the weekend time slots of the pumpkin patch and how many visitors each can still take. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the farm manager's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Slots tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Slots tab and the forecast, and records the farm manager's decision after the manager confirms.
June Caldwell: decides every request and updates the Slots tab by hand.

7. THE FARM MANAGER'S DECISIONS

June Caldwell decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Slots tab say, and the manager decides.

A weekend visit. The staff agent checks that the slot is open and has room for the visitors, and works out the admission at 8 dollars per visitor aged 3 and over. The manager reserves or declines. A weekend slot above capacity is an exception, and the manager decides it with a note.
After a reserved visit: the manager lowers visitors_left for that slot by the number of visitors on the Slots tab, and writes full in status when it reaches 0.

A group visit. The staff agent checks that the group has 20 or more visitors, that the date is a Friday in the pumpkin season, at least 7 days away, and works out the price at 6 dollars per person. The manager books or declines. A group visit on less than 7 days' notice is an exception, and the manager decides it with a note.

A weekend forecast. When the manager asks about the weather, the staff agent calls get_forecast and reports the chance of rain, the rain and snow in inches, and the high temperature for each day. The forecast covers the next 16 days, so the manager looks at the coming weekends. On a clear, mild weekend the manager schedules more staff. On a day with heavy rain, snow or ice, the manager closes the farm, writing closed in the status of that day's slots on the Slots tab by hand, and the staff agent drafts a message to the visitors with a reservation.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_slots before answering any question about weekend visit times or room in a slot, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a weekend visit, collect the visitor's name, the slot_id, the number of visitors aged 3 and over and a phone number. For a group, collect the leader's name, the group, the date, the number of visitors and a phone number. Repeat the details back.
When the visitor says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the farm confirms every reservation. Never say that a visit is reserved.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the visitor's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the manager asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_slots when the manager asks whether a visit fits, and get_forecast for the weather.
Record a decision only when the manager names a request_id and says reserve, book or decline. Repeat it, ask the manager to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a reserved visit, remind the manager to lower visitors_left on the Slots tab.
