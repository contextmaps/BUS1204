COPPER LADLE FOOD TRUCK, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM03. Copper Ladle Food Truck is made up, and so are the customers, events and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Copper Ladle Food Truck parks at a different stop in Blacksburg or Christiansburg each weekday and caters events on weekends. Customers preorder meals for pickup at a stop, and groups book catering at least 14 days ahead. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a preorder for a stop, and a catering request. Your staff agent works with Tomas Reyes, the owner, who decides every request. The truck does not open when thunderstorms or snow are forecast during service, so your staff agent checks the forecast, and the owner uses it to close a stop and to plan how much food to prepare.

The system has five parts:
Stops tab: this week's stops and how many more preorder meals the kitchen can prepare for each. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the owner's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Stops tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Stops tab and the forecast, and records the owner's decision after the owner confirms.
Tomas Reyes: decides every request and updates the Stops tab by hand.

7. THE OWNER'S DECISIONS

Tomas Reyes decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Stops tab say, and the owner decides.

A preorder. The staff agent checks that the stop is open, that it has enough meals left, that the preorder holds 10 meals or fewer, and that it came in by 9:00 am for lunch or 2:00 pm for dinner on the day of the stop. The owner confirms or declines. A preorder after the cutoff or of more than 10 meals is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.
After a confirmed preorder: the owner lowers meals_left for that stop by the number of meals on the Stops tab, and writes full in status when it reaches 0.

A catering request. The staff agent checks that the event is on a Saturday or Sunday, for 25 to 150 people, at least 14 days away, and works out the price at 14 dollars per person and the 100 dollar deposit. The owner books or declines. Catering on less than 14 days' notice is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.

A stormy day. When the owner asks about the weather, the staff agent calls get_forecast and reports the chance of rain, any thunderstorm or snow code, and the high temperature for each day. The forecast covers the next 16 days, so the owner looks at the coming stops. On a day with thunderstorms or snow forecast, the owner closes the stop by hand, writing closed in its status on the Stops tab, and the staff agent drafts a message to the customers with a confirmed preorder for that stop.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_stops before answering any question about where the truck is, its hours or open preorders, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For a preorder, collect the customer's name, the stop_id, the meals and how many, and a phone number. For catering, collect the group's name, the event date, the number of guests and a phone number. Repeat the details back.
When the customer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the owner confirms every request. Never say that a preorder or an event is confirmed.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the customer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the owner asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_stops when the owner asks whether a preorder fits, and get_forecast for the weather.
Record a decision only when the owner names a request_id and says confirm, book or decline. Repeat it, ask the owner to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a confirmed preorder, remind the owner to lower meals_left on the Stops tab.
