TEAM10. Petalcrest Flowers is made up, and so are the customers, couples and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Petalcrest Flowers is a florist in Radford that delivers arrangements in Radford, Christiansburg, Fairlawn, Blacksburg and Pulaski, and designs flowers for weddings. Orders placed by 12:00 pm are delivered the same day, and holiday orders close 3 days before the holiday. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: a delivery order, and a wedding or event inquiry. Your staff agent works with Grace Okafor, the owner, who decides every request. Snow, ice and heat change how many deliveries the drivers can make, so your staff agent checks the forecast, and the owner uses it to plan drivers and to close deliveries on a day with ice or heavy snow.

The system has five parts:
Deliveries tab: the delivery days of the week, how many more deliveries each can take, and the order cutoff. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the owner's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Deliveries tab and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Deliveries tab and the forecast, and records the owner's decision after the owner confirms.
Grace Okafor: decides every request and updates the Deliveries tab by hand.

7. THE OWNER'S DECISIONS

Grace Okafor decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Deliveries tab say, and the owner decides.

An order. The staff agent checks that the day is open and has deliveries left, that the order came in before the day's cutoff, that the town is one the shop delivers to, that the recipient is not in a hospital's intensive care unit, and works out the price with the delivery fee (12 dollars in Radford, Christiansburg and Fairlawn, 20 dollars in Blacksburg and Pulaski) and any holiday price. The owner confirms or declines. A same day order after 12:00 pm or a holiday order after the cutoff is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.
After a confirmed order: the owner lowers deliveries_left for that day by 1 on the Deliveries tab, and writes full in status when it reaches 0.

A wedding. The staff agent checks that the date is at least 8 weeks away, and notes the starting price of 1,200 dollars and the 50 percent deposit. The owner sets a meeting with the designer, named with the time in the note, or declines. A wedding on less than 8 weeks' notice is an exception, and the owner decides it with a note.

A snowy or icy week. When the owner asks about the weather, the staff agent calls get_forecast and reports the low temperature, the chance of snow, the snowfall and any freezing rain code for each day. The forecast covers the next 16 days, so the owner looks at the coming delivery days. On a day with ice or heavy snow, the owner closes deliveries, writing closed in that day's status on the Deliveries tab by hand, and the staff agent drafts a message to the customers with a confirmed order for that day.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_deliveries before answering any question about delivery days, cutoffs or holiday prices, and answer only from the rows it receives.
For an order, collect the customer's name, the delivery day, the arrangement, the town, the recipient with the address, the card message and a phone number. For a wedding, collect the couple's names, the date and a phone number. Repeat the details back.
When the customer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once, give the request_id and say that the shop confirms every order. Never say that an order is confirmed.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the customer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the owner asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_deliveries when the owner asks whether an order fits, and get_forecast for the weather.
Record a decision only when the owner names a request_id and says confirm, consult or decline. Repeat it, ask the owner to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a confirmed order, remind the owner to lower deliveries_left on the Deliveries tab.
