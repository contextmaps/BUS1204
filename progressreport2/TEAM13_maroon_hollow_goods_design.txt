MAROON HOLLOW GOODS, SYSTEM DESIGN FOR PROGRESS REPORT 2
TEAM13. Maroon Hollow Goods is made up, and so are the customers and names below. This design builds on your fact sheet from progress report 1 and on the Kestrel Bend system from lec13_1. Every box of text marked COPY is pasted exactly as it is.


1. YOUR BUSINESS IN SHORT

Maroon Hollow Goods is an online shop in Blacksburg that sells Virginia Tech themed apparel and gifts in the United States and to Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan and South Korea. Customers order online and may return items that are not personalized within 30 days. Your customer agent takes two kinds of request: an order, and a return. Your staff agent works with Rachel Kim, the operations manager, who decides every request. Customers abroad see prices in their own currency and pay in dollars, so both agents check the exchange rates, the customer agent to show a price abroad and the manager to quote international orders.

The system has five parts:
Products tab: the products, their prices in dollars and how many are in stock. Both agents read it.
Requests tab: one row per request, with its status and the operations manager's note. Only the staff agent reads it.
Customer agent: reads the Products tab and the exchange rates and adds a request to the Requests tab. It does not hold the office key.
Staff agent: lists the requests, checks the Products tab and the exchange rates, and records the operations manager's decision after the manager confirms.
Rachel Kim: decides every request and updates the Products tab by hand.

7. THE OPERATIONS MANAGER'S DECISIONS

Rachel Kim decides every request. Before each decision, the staff agent checks what the fact sheet and the Products tab say, and the manager decides.

An order. The staff agent checks that the product and the size are in stock, that the country is one the shop ships to, and works out the total: the item price, 12 dollars for a name and number, and shipping (standard 6 dollars and free from 75 dollars, express 18 dollars, abroad 25 dollars). For an order abroad, the staff agent also gives the total in the customer's currency from get_rates and notes that the customer pays any import tax. The manager confirms or declines.
After a confirmed order: the manager lowers stock for that product by the quantity on the Products tab, and writes sold out in status when it reaches 0.

A return. The staff agent checks that the item is not personalized, that it is unworn with tags, and that it came within 30 days of delivery, and works out the refund of the item price. Return shipping is free in the United States and paid by the customer abroad. The manager marks the return refunded, with the amount in the note, or declines. A return after 30 days, a refund of shipping, or a return of a personalized item is an exception, and the manager decides it with a note.
After a refunded return: the manager raises stock for that product by 1 on the Products tab when the item can be sold again.

The exchange rates. When the manager asks about the rates, the staff agent calls get_rates and reports how many Canadian dollars, pounds, euros, yen and won one dollar buys today, and the prices of the products in those currencies. The prices in the Products tab stay in dollars, and every order is charged in dollars.

9. WHAT YOUR INSTRUCTIONS NEED

Your agents now write to and read from the sheet, so your team updates the SCORE instructions it wrote for progress report 1. Write them yourselves, on the pattern of the Kestrel Bend instructions in lec13_1. The add_request call takes the place of the HANDOFF line in the customer agent, and decide_request takes the place of the RECORD line in the staff agent.

The customer agent's instructions need to say:
Call read_products before answering any question about products, prices, sizes or stock, and answer only from the rows it receives. Call get_rates when a customer asks for a price in another currency, and give it as an estimate, charged in dollars.
For an order, collect the customer's name, email, the product, size, quantity, any name and number, the country and the shipping, one product per request. For a return, collect the name, email, the product, size, the order it came from and the reason with the days since delivery. Repeat the details back.
When the customer says yes, call add_request in the same reply, once for each product, give each request_id and say that the shop confirms every order and return. Never say that an order is confirmed or a refund given.
The agent cannot see other requests, and says it can help only with the customer's own new request.

The staff agent's instructions need to say:
Call list_requests when the manager asks about requests, with status pending for what is waiting, and report each request with its request_id and details.
Call read_products when the manager asks whether an order fits, and get_rates for prices abroad.
Record a decision only when the manager names a request_id and says confirm, refund or decline. Repeat it, ask the manager to confirm, then call decide_request once.
After a confirmed order or a refunded return, remind the manager to update stock on the Products tab.
